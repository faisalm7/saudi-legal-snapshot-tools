# Source rights and public-delivery status

Reviewed 23 October 2026. **The public data release is a 468-row factual BOE source catalogue; zero examined legal-text records are eligible for this delivery.** No legal bodies, article text, PDF extracts or judgment facts are distributed. Publishing source links, titles, dates, hashes and bounded analytic diagnostics is a different decision from publishing the underlying captured content.

MIT applies to the newly authored code, documentation and invented fixtures. CC0 applies to the publisher's original catalogue compilation and analytic contributions only, to the extent the publisher can waive or dedicate those rights. It does not claim ownership of official source facts or grant rights over source texts, source websites, third-party material or dependencies. The source catalogue's license is not a source-text license. Private document records leave `rights_basis` null.

The factual catalogue has a separate affirmative basis: Article 4(1) of the current Saudi Copyright Law excludes bare facts, and the release uses its own schema and arrangement of public identifiers, descriptive instrument names, dates, hashes and independently derived measurements. It does not reproduce legal bodies, article headings, editorial summaries or BOE's creative portal arrangement. No ownership is asserted over the underlying facts. The limited metadata does not automatically inherit the full-text hold. [Current law](https://www.uqn.gov.sa/details?p=28845).

The law also excludes official legal documents and addresses public-domain use while retaining circulation rules. The earlier implementing regulation contained an official-publication approval provision. The new regulation is established, but the applicable transition/circulation rule and the terms governing these captured copies have not been resolved sufficiently to support the proposed **full-text derivative**. That hold does not assert that the old rule definitely survives, that public documents are secret or that lack of an open-license label defeats a sufficient statutory basis. [New regulation](https://www.uqn.gov.sa/decisions-and-regulations/4001498), [older regulation](https://www.uqn.gov.sa/details?p=19857).

The BOE terms' general personal-use language creates contextual contractual uncertainty; it is not treated as an affirmative open-data license. The examined terms contain no express outbound factual-catalogue or metadata-reuse prohibition, and the limited catalogue has been assessed on that narrower scope. This does not clear renewed scraping, different content or unrestricted reuse of linked documents. [BOE terms](https://www.boe.gov.sa/ar/Pages/TermsOfUse.aspx).

## Separate publication gate

A supported decision must identify the exact document ID, source-file and text hashes, rights basis, HTTPS evidence URLs, reasoning, and quality/privacy outcomes. A clear applicable source-wide grant may be cited where it actually covers the record. `select_supported` validates the registry mechanically; it does not verify the truth of its claims. `prepare-public` stops before creating output when none qualify and never overwrites an existing destination. The supplied example registry is empty.

Private processing, a hash, `license: other`, gated access, a pattern scan and publisher confirmation cannot manufacture missing permissions or establish anonymity. Rights, source/version identity, faithful capture and privacy remain independent reviews. The catalogue projection is not a way to bypass the text gate: it exports no bodies or article rows. No source owner was contacted during this review.
