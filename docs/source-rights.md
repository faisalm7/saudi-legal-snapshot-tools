Reviewed 23 October 2026. The selected text release has an affirmative source-specific publication basis. Earlier automatic holds in the tools-only release are superseded for this reviewed subset; neither processing success nor a missing licence label supplies the basis.

**BOE official wording.** The [current M/169 Copyright Law](https://www.uqn.gov.sa/details?p=28845), particularly Articles 1,4(2), 25 and 59–61, excludes official legal documents and places excluded wording in a permission-free public domain. The [current regulation](https://www.uqn.gov.sa/decisions-and-regulations/4001498), including verified provisions 29(3)/30(5) in their stated contexts, supports statutory uses and public-domain republication. The reviewed [BOE terms](https://www.boe.gov.sa/ar/Pages/TermsOfUse.aspx) supplied no identified express outbound republication prohibition for these already-public official bodies. The earlier hold treated inability to certify repeal of a historical approval clause as an automatic veto; that disposition was withdrawn. This is an adequately supported publication assessment, **not formal certification of the repeal of every older circulation rule** or a declaration that all contracts/circulation rules are void.

**MOJ official publications.** The [MOJ open-data policy](https://www.moj.gov.sa/ar/OpenData/Pages/OpenDataPolicy.aspx) covers public unprotected Ministry information and expressly includes judgments/precedents; it is not limited to statistics. Its source-wide grant covers the matched official publications without invented per-record permission requirements. Retain Ministry/source attribution and source wording/integrity. Observe the policy's prohibition on political, racist or unlawful uses and its other stated use/responsibility conditions. The [general terms](https://www.moj.gov.sa/ar/Ministry/Pages/TermsOfUse.aspx) are read with this applicable open-data exception. Official legal wording also has the statutory exclusion above. Faithful conversion and documented extraction corrections do not authorize substantive rewriting, invented anonymization or use of protected third-party additions.

Only reviewed official bodies, genuine source headings/units, factual metadata and documented faithful conversions are included. Creative commentary, private translations/standards, website design/logos, source assets and original facsimiles are outside this grant/assessment. SAMA, Qadha, grievance compilations and other inspected families do not automatically inherit MOJ/BOE permission and contribute no bodies to this release.

Rights are separate from source/version fidelity and privacy. The court subset underwent full-context decisions; public access and publisher masking were not treated as consent or guaranteed anonymity. Exact source/text/projection hashes and supported decisions bind the selected records.
