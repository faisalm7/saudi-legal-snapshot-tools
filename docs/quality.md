# Quality and selection

The release has 1,451 source publication records, not a deduplicated count of unique laws/cases. Exact configuration counts, native-processing denominators and file hashes are in the dataset’s `metrics.json`; reviewed body/metadata/structure bindings are in `record-reviews.jsonl`.

## BOE: 457 bodies and 9,150 article spans

All 468 JSON/PDF pairs were processed. The frozen v0.2 had 9,305 spans in 350 documents; refinement produced 9,353 spans in 352 input bodies after source-backed split-word/false-heading repairs. The selected release has 346 structured bodies/9,150 spans and 111 body-only records. Eleven material gaps remain: integral health annexes; pesticide sanctions; mining/security-training/patent fees; civil salary schedules; actuarial formulas/tables; the ports instrument body; and normative flag/medal/road-sign illustrations. These are quality deferrals, not an unresolved BOE rights ban. No guessed article rows fill missing structure.

Four original-base bodies have source-image completions: education Article 6 (+715 characters); transport Article 5 (+571); Red Crescent Article 11 (+386); development-bank Article 8 (+741) and Article 9 (+636). New images were captured separately in 2026 and matched to the old version using adjacent wording/enactment witnesses, without mixing later amended membership/delegation clauses. Independent review replayed source-image transcription and all rebased spans. The Red Crescent source's repeated final category numeral 6 remains exactly as printed, with an uncertainty flag. `REPAIRS.json` records exact before/after passages, image URLs/hashes/times, original/new identities and existing PDF heading evidence. Original captures were preserved.

All body hashes and exact selected span slices were checked. The purposive diagnostic covered 50 new documents/150 paired source windows plus eight old targets and a boundary control; 15 page images were inspected, often as crops. The new three restorations added five target image pages and six adjacent/version witnesses. This is not all-page glyph inspection or a population accuracy estimate. At least 12 diagnostic documents lost rendered list numbering; flagged table flattening and upstream paragraph losses remain. All-body screening plus 76 bounded contexts across 50 flagged BOE records found no concrete privacy deferral; this does not mean 468 complete manual reads.

The parent hierarchy preserves 7,014 native source units: 4,505 explicit articles, 1,605 useful other units, 873 empty and 31 punctuation-only nodes. ## Courts: 918 records and 920 source body components

Every proposed publication context was read individually with independent review; the selected full API fields/native PDF transcriptions, numbers/dates/negations, roles and source differences were assessed. The initial 514 source-version contexts for 513 locators and the later 486 distinct current source/body contexts are separate, overlapping review cohorts, not a sum of unique cases. Source hashes and component/projection bindings were checked for every selected row. The final old-hold consistency review reuses exact original source evidence and changes only decisions/faithful component metadata; no private facts or source words are rewritten. 71 current source versions remain private for actual personal financial, family, home, criminal or identifying or unresolved-role contexts. A separately reviewed publisher-masked current version may qualify while an older unmasked PDF remains private.

API and PDF are not interchangeable: PDFs can expose names, docket identifiers, dates or signatures absent/masked in the API, clip text under layout controls, or differ in glyph/numeric order. PDF-only private values are never appended to an API body. Unavailable current PDFs stay null/flagged rather than receiving invented hashes. All available original Supreme/other-court native layers were diagnostically compared; selected whole facsimiles and contextual crops were inspected during the later batches. One reviewer alone checked 49 complete later-record facsimiles/52 pages across 192 contexts; this denominator is not claimed for all later records. Source clipping or an actual replacement-character corruption can defer a body even when software checks pass.

All 29 original historical sources/30 pages were read and visually checked; exact glyph/date/punctuation/negation correction ledgers were replayed. Twenty original PDF byte hashes were independently rechecked; nine bounded cloud-unavailable rechecks explicitly rely on primary builder hash/native/render receipts plus independent full-page inspection. Historical capture time stays null. The one newly acquired current-PDF body was separately checked in full. Four additional historical-native pilot versions were individually reviewed but add zero document coverage because their current API canonicals were already selected.

The complete 38,011-file inventory was byte/native/JSON processed, but this does not clear every body for release. Case-PDF exact copies/raw groups, source locators, source versions and semantic decisions are distinct counting units. The frozen 1,630 current API bodies include 1,141 still pending full contextual review; transport-retry, provenance and outer-boundary queues remain resumable. Qadha authored compilations have unresolved official-text/commentary/reuse boundaries; 17 grievance volumes have no usable native Arabic and the bounded OCR benchmark failed. Neither conclusion is extrapolated to all government judgments.

## Verification limits

The public verifier checks all text/projection hashes, exact article/component slices, native hierarchy membership and decision bindings using public artifacts. It does not fetch original official bytes, certify anonymity or substantive law, establish completeness, or produce an expert accuracy score. Publisher masks do not guarantee anonymity; public-office, professional and actual business roles can remain after contextual assessment. No local privacy redactions, raw judicial HTML, private review contexts or original facsimiles accompany this release. All development and source/independent review were AI assisted.
