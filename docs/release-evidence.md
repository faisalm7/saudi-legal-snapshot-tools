## Structure and repair limits

PDF font mappings can transpose or duplicate lam–alef in article headings, and preambles can cite article numbers. The [heading matcher](https://github.com/faisalm7/saudi-legal-snapshot-tools/blob/71e162d5e0bd2c3a50b24efb0ce44ffb38ba9444/src/saudi_legal/articles.py#L13) uses a permissive alignment key only for structural matching. It accepts isolated PDF headings and requires one complete ordered mapping into the captured body; exported article text remains an exact parent slice. The collection has verified BOE spans in 352 parents and unknown article boundaries in 116. This alignment rule is not a general Arabic word-repair algorithm.

A published difficult-layout example is document `sa-moj-historical-pdf-67c249a745d3787db0b0ef084a1549c4342d8e2ae99ace02b2af1cde823927bd`, in the frozen dataset's `judgments-00155.parquet`, with its [original official citation](https://laws.moj.gov.sa/ar/JudicialDecisionsList/1/C9LZxvguReFcU_3eMSKZ4_ukqGmGXF9M81YvcJdPQv4N1PdJnPVRFFtHcAsSpIh4). Two complete acts occupy one original native field: judgment `[191,9260)`, two line feeds `[9260,9262)`, appeal `[9262,12904)`. The public body contains two components, and four formal appeal-header values remain null. Its [strict lineage validator](https://github.com/faisalm7/saudi-legal-snapshot-tools/blob/71e162d5e0bd2c3a50b24efb0ce44ffb38ba9444/src/saudi_legal/moj.py#L384) checks source intervals and coverage; it does not infer the missing headers or authenticate source wording.

Source-specific transcription decisions preserve source masks, numbers, negations, contradictory statements and uncertain marks. Original coordinates and chronological correction evidence remain separate from search normalization. Exact source, body, component and metadata hashes bind a supplied rights/quality/privacy decision to its exported version; validation establishes consistency, not the truth of that decision. [Architecture decisions](decision-record.md), [quality history](quality.md), [court audit history](court-audit.md), [reproduction limits](reproducibility.md) and [source rights](source-rights.md) retain the fuller qualifications.

## Frozen processing queue

The bounded repair queue contains **720 items: 283 completed dispositions and 437 pending**. Completed dispositions comprise 241 supported/public items, 12 specific privacy holds, seven specific quality holds, one recipient/access disposition and 22 alternate-version dispositions. These queue units are not the dataset's row totals.

The pending partition is five active private batch-56 items, five reserved batch-57 items and 427 unassigned items. The active five have no final eligibility; further source-fidelity review remains pending. The reserved five have no complete batch review or disposition; limited technical preparation and a partial original-source inspection do not count as completion. Pending items are not classified as rejected. No remaining-collection completion or future processing commitment is implied by this release.

The original material was collected in 2025 for an earlier legal AI project. The user directed this release, with Codex assisting in extraction, source comparisons, code and documentation. Independent review denotes a separate AI-agent pass, not human or legal-expert certification. Capture dates do not establish legal force, and the collection is not a current consolidation of Saudi law.
