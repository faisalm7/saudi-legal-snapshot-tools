# Other source families and judgment limits

No judgment, circular, numeric legislation copy, private standard, religious text or commentary is included as source text in this release. The public source catalogue is limited to BOE metadata. The expanded private source audit is separate from the BOE processing totals and does not make these tools a general validated court/MOJ/SAMA extractor.

A PDF file count is not a distinct-case count. Multi-file cases, long compilations, exact-text duplicates, empty capture containers and derived processed JSONs must be reconciled before reporting judgment coverage. Folder names and historical index counts do not establish provenance. A domain-level PDF link or print metadata date does not establish an original document URL, legal issue date or independently verified capture date.

Readable extraction also does not establish fidelity: source captures can omit the substantive decision, and extracted Arabic may have word-order or character-mapping defects. Document-level source/version evidence, rendered comparison and review of identifying/private-party material remain necessary. No case facts, party information, private identifiers or sensitive examples are reproduced here, and no anonymization claim is made.

MOJ's open-data policy is a possible applicable grant and must be assessed alongside generic terms. A source-wide license can cover a matched set of records where its scope is established. The present judgment copies have not passed the combined rights, identity, fidelity and privacy gate. SAMA Rulebook locators likewise improve tracing without overriding the terms incorporated by that source.

Hadith and AAOIFI material remain outside the project release scope. Journal studies, commentary and unofficial translations must not inherit rights from an official legal-text label. The source-family review supports careful deferral and useful private work, rather than a claim that the whole collection is unusable or that the easiest BOE inputs prove the best public corpus.


The expanded investigation successfully read and privately hashed **242 selected original files: 140 PDFs and 102 historical JSONs, covering 2,468 PDF pages**. These comprise all 70 selected MOJ legislation PDFs, all 20 SAMA Rulebook prints, a separate circular, 47 case PDFs and two distinct scanned judgment volumes, plus paired historical extractions. Bounded 60–90 second reads supersede the earlier three-second availability diagnostic for these files; every selected expanded read succeeded. This is not a whole-collection availability estimate or a claim that every extracted page was visually inspected.

The 47 case PDFs have distinct file hashes but form **35 exact raw-text groups**, with 12 excess copies. Four empty captures share a portal-shell group; substantive text forms 34 groups. Current source recovery produced 24 successful detail responses, including a rejected wrong-year candidate, and exposed differences between captured and current masking. Four full current bodies were compared against seven rendered captured-source pages. Privacy patterns returning zero hits do not establish anonymity, and no private-party material or judgment locators appear here.

Actual official public searches recovered title-and-issuance-date candidates for **all 70 MOJ legislation copies**; 68 uniquely also match their printed publication date, while two publication discrepancies remain. Four current detail responses provide real statute/version/section IDs; full body/version identity and permission remain separate gates. **All 20 SAMA prints** contain observed canonical native PDF links, and three fresh native PDFs were recovered, but extractor-dependent font-map defects remain. Full PDFium extraction of 128 native original PDFs took 4.455 aggregate seconds on this machine, excluding hydration; the 70 MOJ PDFs took 2.757 seconds. A local OCR benchmark covered six scanbook pages at 200 dpi plus four additional 300 dpi configurations. Four clearly printed date anchors on one interior page survived exactly 0/4 in each compared configuration, so processing was not scaled merely because OCR was fast. No non-BOE source text was cleared for public upload by this expanded investigation.
